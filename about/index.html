Bio

I'm Gregory O'Connor, an MIT-trained Ph.D., product builder, strategist, and co-founder of Socure. I live and work in New York, and I run Vociferous AI, where I build AI and web applications and advise ventures across product, market, brand, and financial strategy.

Most of the work starts before there is a product: a founder or team has a real opportunity, partial information, and pressure to decide. I help determine whether the opportunity should become software, build a testable first version, and stay with it through evaluation, launch, and handoff. Because I also handle the market, brand, and financial analysis, technical choices stay connected to the business they have to serve.

Education

Ph.D., Department of Chemical Engineering, Massachusetts Institute of Technology, in the MIT Biotechnology Center, with a minor in Artificial Intelligence. B.S., Vanderbilt University, with a triple major in Biomedical Engineering, Chemistry, and Mathematics.
