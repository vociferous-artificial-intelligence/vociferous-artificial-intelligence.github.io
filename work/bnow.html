BNOW.NET — source-grounded public intelligence

What this is

BNOW.NET is an application for analysts working with fast-moving public information. This page describes the public product direction without disclosing private sources, credentials, ingestion details, or internal analysis.

The problem

When events move quickly, the difficulty is not finding another stream of updates. It is keeping claims connected to their sources, comparing evidence across channels, and preserving enough context for a person to decide what deserves attention.

The product direction

BNOW.NET is built around source-grounded analysis: organize public information, retain links back to evidence, and give an analyst a clearer path from an emerging signal to the material behind it. Human judgment remains responsible for interpretation.

A future relationship to Epistemic

BNOW.NET may become a data source for future Epistemic analysis and provides a concrete defense-oriented example of the kinds of high-accountability systems that work may support. The projects are separate today; this is a possible future relationship, not a claim of current integration.

What this illustrates

Data-intensive applications need product judgment as much as infrastructure. Source quality, evidence presentation, analyst workflow, uncertainty, and the boundaries of automation all have to be designed together.
